ABOUT THE PROJECT

A smarter front door to company knowledge.

This application lets people ask questions about policies, SOPs, HR manuals, technical documents, and internal knowledge without hunting through folders.

The pipeline uses Flask, LangChain, ChromaDB, Ollama, nomic-embed-text, gemma:2b, and retrieval-augmented generation.
